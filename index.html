Helio

A private, offline point of sale for iPhone and iPad.

Coming soon to the App Store

Sell, track inventory, manage customers, and print or share receipts — all on your device. Your store lives in a single .helio file that you own. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Features

  • Products, optional inventory, customers, taxes and currency
  • Split payments, refunds and optional cash-register sessions
  • Image and PDF receipts, AirPrint and Bluetooth thermal printers
  • Sales analytics and an on-device assistant powered by Apple Intelligence
  • Import existing records from CSV and SQLite files
  • Available in English and Spanish
