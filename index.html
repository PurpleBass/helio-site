FAQ

What everyone asks.

Do I need internet to use Helio?

No. Helio sells, saves orders, prints and links your team without internet. You'll only need it for online features coming soon, like taking cards or sending documents to sign.

Do I have to create an account?

No. Open Helio, create your store and start selling. We don't ask for an email or password.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, and Macs with Apple silicon too. There's no Android version for now.

My products are in Excel. Can I bring them over?

Yes. Import products and customers from CSV files (what Excel or Google Sheets save) or SQLite. Helio includes ready-to-fill templates.

Which printers can I use?

AirPrint printers and Bluetooth thermal receipt printers (from the Float plan). You can also send the receipt as an image or PDF by WhatsApp or email.

How many people can use my store?

From Float you can invite your team with a QR code and give each person a role and permissions. Your staff don't pay: joining a store is free.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your stores still open so you can view and export everything in them. Only new sales pause until you're back on a plan. We never take your data away.

Does Helio issue tax invoices (CFDI)?

Not yet: Helio's receipts are not tax invoices. CFDI 4.0 invoicing from Helio is on its way.

What if I lose my iPhone?

Your store is a file you can back up wherever you like (iCloud Drive, your computer or a USB drive), and Helio keeps automatic versions so you can go back to an earlier copy.

How do I cancel?

In your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Helio. Cancel during the free month and you won't be charged.
