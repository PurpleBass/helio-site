Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

In short: Helio does not sell or share your data, and has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. Your store lives on your device, in files you control. Harcode's servers only see what is needed for optional features you choose to use — documents sent to be signed (encrypted with a key our servers never receive), card payments through Stripe, and new-order notifications. Card numbers never reach Harcode. Subscriptions are handled by Apple.

This policy (which also serves as our privacy notice under Mexico's Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares) explains how Helio (the "App"), published by Harcode ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on iPhone, iPad and Mac, to its online services and to this website.

1. Who is responsible

Harcode is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at support@harcode.com for any question or request about your data.

2. Information we collect

The App has no user accounts and does not send your products, sales, customers or settings to Harcode or to any third party. The exceptions are optional features you choose to use:

  • a document you send for signature (section 8), which passes through our service encrypted;
  • card payments through Stripe (section 9); and
  • new-order notifications (section 10).

3. Information the App handles on your device

To work as a point of sale, the App stores the information you enter, such as products, prices, inventory, sales, refunds, payments recorded, register sessions, orders, suppliers, expenses, coupons and loyalty records, and customer details (for example names, phone numbers, email and postal addresses, delivery locations and notes). This information is saved:

  • in a .helio store file, in the App's Documents folder or in a location you choose in the Files app; and
  • for App preferences (such as printer and receipt settings), in the App's local settings on your device.

If you keep your store file in a cloud location such as iCloud Drive, that copy is handled by that provider under its own privacy policy (for iCloud, Apple's Privacy Policy).

4. Why we use information

The only purposes for which Harcode handles personal data are: providing the optional features you turn on (signing, card payments, notifications); confirming that a subscription is active; answering your support requests; and meeting legal obligations. We do not use your data for marketing, profiling or advertising, and we do not sell it.

5. Your customers' information

If you record information about your own customers or employees, you are responsible for it, including giving them your own privacy notice, obtaining any consent and meeting the data-protection laws that apply to your business (in Mexico, the Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares). Harcode does not receive or process that information, except encrypted signing documents and payment records as described below, which we handle on your behalf.

6. Permissions

  • Bluetooth — only to find and print to thermal receipt printers you pair with.
  • Add to Photos — only when you choose to save a receipt or order image. The App cannot read your photos.
  • Camera — only while you scan a barcode, a loyalty card or the invitation code of a store you join. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Local network — only to link devices of the same store that are near each other.
  • Location — only for Tap to Pay on iPhone, because Stripe requires the iPhone's location to accept cards. It is used while you take a payment and is not kept by Harcode.
  • Notifications — only to alert you of new orders and of a card payment that did not go through.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Declining them only disables the related feature.

7. Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are bought and billed by Apple. Harcode does not receive your name, Apple Account, card or billing details. The App checks on your device, through Apple's StoreKit, which plan is active.

8. Documents sent for signature

When you send a document to be signed, the App encrypts it on your device and uploads it to Harcode's signing service, which runs on Cloudflare. The key is part of the link you share and never reaches our servers, so neither Harcode nor Cloudflare can read the document or what the signer writes or attaches.

To give you a record of the signature, the service keeps, unencrypted, the times the link was created, opened and signed, and the IP address it was signed from. Everything is deleted from the service as soon as the App collects the signature, when you cancel the link, and in any case 30 days after it was created.

9. Card payments with Stripe

If you choose to accept cards, the App connects your store to Stripe, Inc., our payment processor, through Harcode's payment service (which runs on Cloudflare). Stripe processes those payments under the Stripe Privacy Policy.

  • Setting up your account. We send Stripe the email address you enter and your store's name and address to create your Stripe account. Identity and bank details (such as your CLABE) are entered directly on Stripe's pages; Harcode never receives them. Harcode keeps your Stripe account identifier so your payments go to your account.
  • Taking a payment. With Tap to Pay on iPhone, the card is read by Apple and Stripe on your iPhone; the card number never reaches the App or Harcode. Our service sees the amount, currency, the payment's identifier and its result, which it needs to create and confirm the charge and Helio's fee. Payment links work the same way: the customer pays on Stripe's page.

Apple may collect information to provide Tap to Pay on iPhone, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

10. New-order notifications

To alert you when a new order arrives, Harcode's service keeps your device's Apple push-notification token together with your store's identifier, only to send those alerts. You can stop them at any time by turning notifications off for Helio in iOS Settings.

11. Assistant

The App's assistant uses Apple's on-device language model. Your questions and store data are processed on your device and are not sent to Harcode. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles Apple Intelligence.

12. Linking devices and usage on your device

If you invite someone to your store, their device connects directly to yours over the local network or Wi-Fi Aware; store data travels between those devices only, never through Harcode. The App also counts which screens you use so you can see it in More › Usage; that count stays on your device.

13. Sharing and transfers

We do not transfer your personal data to third parties, except to the providers that run the optional features above on our behalf (Cloudflare for hosting our services, Stripe for payments, Apple for notifications and purchases), and when required by law or a competent authority. These transfers do not require your consent under Mexican law. Actions you start yourself — sharing a store file or receipt, sending an email, printing, or opening a contact in WhatsApp or Maps — hand information to that app or service under its own policy.

14. Tracking, advertising and this website

The App does not track you across apps or websites, does not show ads, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. The only third-party SDK is Stripe's, used solely to take card payments. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical data such as your IP address for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

15. Security and retention

Your data is protected by the security of your device; we recommend a device passcode and regular backups. Documents sent for signature are encrypted end to end with AES-256-GCM. Your data stays until you delete it: delete a store file from the App or the Files app, and deleting the App removes the data in its own folder. We keep Stripe identifiers and payment records only while you use card payments and as long as the law requires.

16. Your rights (ARCO)

You may access, rectify or cancel your personal data held by Harcode, oppose its use, revoke your consent or limit its use or disclosure. Send your request to support@harcode.com with your name, the right you want to exercise, and enough information to locate the data (for example your store's identifier or the payment involved). We will answer within 20 business days, and if your request is granted we will act on it within the following 15 business days. If you believe your rights were not respected, you may go to Mexico's data-protection authority.

17. Children

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

18. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.

19. Contact

Harcode — support@harcode.com
